1

Collect the documents

We start from each hospital's financial assistance web page and collect its policy, plain-language summary and application.

Scanned pages are converted to text.

2

AI reads everything

The AI reads all of a hospital's documents at once and answers 18 questions based on the law.

Every answer must quote the document it came from.

3

Fact-check the AI

Software confirms every quote appears word for word in the hospital's documents, and checks figures against official sources.

Anything it can't confirm goes to a person.

4

Grade by fixed rules

The letter grade comes from a fixed formula, never from the AI.

Given CSSNY's answers, it gives the same grade CSSNY gave for all 211 hospitals.

5

Publish with evidence

Each hospital gets a grade and a plain-language explanation of each problem, citing the law.

Every finding links to the exact page quoted.
